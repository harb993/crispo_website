drumstick.glb, drumstick-lite.glb

This work is based on "fried chicken drumstick 3d model"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fried-chicken-drumstick-3d-model-d9cf856b309246dea408a519ca37a91d)
by grodan (https://sketchfab.com/zhaoqinzzq), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: re-oriented; simplified from 1.39M to 300k / 110k triangles; ambient
occlusion baked into vertex colours; colour regraded; normal, roughness and
clearcoat maps derived; geometry (meshopt) and textures (WebP) compressed.
Produced with web/tools/prepare-model.mjs.
